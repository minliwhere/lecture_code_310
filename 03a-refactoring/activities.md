## Ticket FEAT-0002

The loyalty scheme is being expanded. A customer's status comes from how many distinct program categories they have taken:

- 3 or more categories → "Frequent Customer" (already the rule)
- 5 or more → "Community Champion" (new)
- Going on a waitlist counts too!

## Extend FEAT-0002 (Refactor First)

The loyalty scheme is being expanded AGAIN! When a customer has joined 4 program categories (registered or waitlisted), their status should be “Community Enthusiast”.

1. Analyze: what would it cost to implement this change in the current version? Why?
